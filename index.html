Marvel Studios Top 5

Recommended by your baby

1. Avengers: Infinity War

⭐ 5/5 • Action & Sci-Fi

It's my favorite movie of the entire franchise. It perfectly combines great special effects, the right amount of humor, non-stop action, an amazing plot, and an ending that literally gives you goosebumps!

▶ WATCH ON DISNEY+

2. Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3

⭐ 5/5 • Sci-Fi & Adventure

An absolutely excellent way to conclude the Guardians of the Galaxy trilogy. I've only watched this movie once and it genuinely made me cry in the theater that day.

▶ WATCH ON DISNEY+

3. Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness

⭐ 4/5 • Action & Fantasy

I found it super interesting how Wanda turns from hero to villain, but in my opinion, I don't really consider her a villain, just someone who has deep feelings. The movie is good and features great effects.

▶ WATCH ON DISNEY+

4. Spider-Man: No Way Home

⭐ 5/5 • Action & Heroes

It's really interesting how they bring the 3 Spider-Men together for this movie. It has great humor and also an ending that feels completely fair for Peter Parker.

▶ WATCH ON DISNEY+
Recommended by your baby

5. Thor: Ragnarok

⭐ 5/5 • Comedy & Action

This movie made me laugh so much, it's incredibly good! The villain is exceptionally well played and she is definitely one of my favorites from Marvel Studios.

▶ WATCH ON DISNEY+
